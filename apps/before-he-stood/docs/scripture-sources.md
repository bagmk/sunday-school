## Method

- Korean primary source: the publisher's own reader, 대한성서공회 (Korean Bible Society) `bskorea.or.kr/bible/korbibReadpage.php` with `version=GAE` (= 개역개정); every fetched page displayed the label 개역개정. Second source: churchofjesuschrist.org Korean scriptures (`lang=kor`), whose text matches 개역개정 wording (작은 자야, 신성을 모독하도다, 권능) rather than 개역한글 (소자야, 참람하도다, 권세). The LDS site is a separately published digital edition, which is what is needed to catch transcription errors. (Searches surfaced holybible.or.kr only for other versions and did not surface godpia; the nocr.net / hangl.net 개역개정 pages refuse automated fetching.)
- English primary source: esv.org (Crossway). Second sources: blueletterbible.org, biblia.com (Faithlife), bible.com (YouVersion), bibleserver.com, biblestudytools.com, biblehub.com/bible.cc. Matt 7:29 and John 9:2–3 were checked on two non-Crossway sites (biblia.com + bible.cc; blueletterbible.org + biblia.com).
- Tooling: the fetch tool's summarizer will not reproduce long copyrighted passages verbatim. Verification therefore used (a) supplying the candidate text and asking for a character-by-character comparison against the fetched page ("EXACT MATCH" or the listed differences), (b) short verbatim segments, and (c) character/space counts for disputed Korean verses. Line breaks in ESV poetry were treated as single spaces; footnote markers were ignored.
- Control test (esv.org Matt 9:1–8) with planted errors: a missing "And", "son" for "my son", a comma moved outside a closing quote, an added comma, and "man" for "men" — all five were caught. The Korean comparisons likewise caught one-syllable and spacing differences (Ps 41:4, Ps 22:1, Lev 24:16, 1 Kgs 8:39, Matt 26:65).
- ESV 2025 check: Crossway, "ESV Text Edition: 2025 Complete List of Changes from 2016", https://uploads.crossway.org/excerpt/esv-2025-text-changes.pdf — none of Matt 9:1–8, 7:29, 8:20, 8:26, 8:34, 26:65; John 9:2–3; Ps 22:1, 38:3, 41:4, 103:3, 107:17, 107:20, 107:29; Deut 6:4; Isa 43:25; Lev 4:31, 24:16; 2 Sam 12:13; 1 Kgs 8:39 is listed (text or punctuation). Nearest listed changes: Matt 9:29, John 9:22–23.
- Typography: esv.org uses curly quotes (“ ” ‘ ’) and em dashes (—); the data stores them as literal UTF-8 characters. Korean 개역개정 has no punctuation.
- Divine name: printed ESV sets it in small capitals (Lᴏʀᴅ). Plain text cannot carry small caps, so the data writes "LORD" (as blueletterbible.org does). esv.org's extracted text shows "Lord" only because the small-caps styling is lost in extraction. Keys affected: PS_41_4, DT_6_4, LV_4_31B (full), LV_24_16A, SA2_12_13B.
- Quote conventions: speech keys (jesus/scribes) hold the spoken words without enclosing quotation marks. Narrator keys hold the published text, including inner quotation marks (MT_9_3, MT_9_4, PS_41_4, and inside some `full` values).
